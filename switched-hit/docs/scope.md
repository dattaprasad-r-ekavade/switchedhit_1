# Project Scope

## In Scope

- **Manager Creation:** Users can create a manager profile with a name and nationality.
- **Team Creation:** Managers can create a new T20 team, specifying the team name and home ground.
- **Home Ground Setup:** Basic home ground selection from a predefined list of stadiums.
- **Player Management:**
    - Auto-generation of a squad of 15 players with random skills and attributes upon team creation.
    - View player profiles and stats.
- **Training Section:**
    - A simple training system to improve player attributes.
    - Managers can assign players to specific training regimes (e.g., batting, bowling, fielding).
- **League Placement:**
    - Teams are automatically placed in a division-based league structure.
- **Game Simulation:**
    - Scheduled, non-real-time game simulations based on a predefined league fixture list.
    - Match results will be determined by a simplified simulation engine based on team and player stats.

## Out of Scope

- **Real-time game simulation/play-by-play.**
- **Player transfers and auctions.**
- **Multiplayer/Online leagues against other users.**
- **Advanced financial management (sponsorships, ticket sales).**
- **In-depth player morale and contract systems.**
- **Customizable logos and kits.**
